Products

SOS — Secretarial Operative System

A turnkey AI layer for the daily secretarial and admin work of professional firms: law firms, accounting practices, architecture studios.

SOS reads and classifies incoming email and WhatsApp, files attachments into the right case folder, drafts contextual replies and tracks deadlines, tasks and appointments. It sits on top of the tools your firm already uses, and keeps a human in the loop on anything sensitive.

01

Intake

Captures and classifies every email and WhatsApp message by urgency and case file, files attachments automatically, and flags anything it cannot classify for manual review.

02

Documentation

Extracts key data with OCR, checks documents for missing signatures or inconsistencies, and keeps the case library versioned and GDPR-compliant.

03

Operation & oversight

Proposes reply drafts, deadlines and meeting prep for human approval, and reports weekly on workload, risk and bottlenecks. Nothing is sent or executed without sign-off.

Recoverable time Up to 32 h / month
Response cycle 48 h → 2 h
Availability 8/5 → 24/7
